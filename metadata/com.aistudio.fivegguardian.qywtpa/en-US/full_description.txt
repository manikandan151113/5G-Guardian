5G Guardian is a lightweight, 100% offline, privacy-first Android utility that continuously monitors your active mobile cellular connection and alerts you when your network degrades or falls back from 5G to 4G LTE or older generations.

Key Features:
* Real-time 5G SA/NSA and LTE network detection
* Screen-off smart battery optimization (alarms automatically paused when screen is unlit)
* Active phone call suppression and 30-second post-call cooldown period
* Zero internet permission requested — 100% offline and zero data collection
* Customizable continuous alarm sirens or gentle single notification tones
* Native Android system sounds and SAF storage custom audio file picker
* Built-in interactive test simulator to safely verify fallback alarms
